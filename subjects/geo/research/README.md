# Geography — evidence and study choices

Reviewed main-paper metadata: 2023–2026. The 2027 syllabus/specimen is the format reference. This is not an all-subject ten-year frequency audit.

## Study route

Prepare five compact theory units thoroughly, add Climate as a sixth option, and keep the larger units to a compulsory-question review. The specimen offers nine theory questions; five answers are required.

The workload ranking comes from the breadth of the 2027 syllabus, not measured page counts in your textbook. Agriculture contains many crop-specific conditions and methods; Manufacturing has several industry/location chains; Minerals/Energy combines deposits, uses and multiple energy sources. The five core units have more compact comparison/reason lists. Individual strengths can change that ranking.

## Full long-answer preparation

- Soils: formation and characteristics of alluvial, black, red and laterite soils; distribution, erosion and conservation.

- Natural vegetation: climate, adaptation, distribution, examples/uses and conservation. Learn reasons, not just tree lists.

- Water resources: irrigation methods, where each fits, advantages and limitations, rainwater harvesting and groundwater conservation.

- Transport: road, rail, water and air comparisons; selection for a given route/cargo; limitations and geographical reasons.

- Waste management: impacts, segregation, treatment/disposal methods, 3Rs and examples of safe handling.

## Backup preparation

- Climate: monsoon mechanisms, seasonal and regional rainfall reasons, temperature controls and climate-table calculations. Choose Minerals/Energy instead only if your own timed answers are already stronger there.

## Lighter compulsory review

- Agriculture; Manufacturing Industries; Minerals and Energy: avoid deep long-answer memorisation initially. Make a short sheet of crop conditions/seasons, major locations, raw materials, energy distinctions and major industrial-location reasons for compulsory items.

- Part I remains compulsory: daily topographical skills, India location practice and whole-syllabus MCQs. These text-only mocks do not replace practice on an actual map sheet.

## Evidence anchors

- 2026 Q1–Q3: Compulsory topo interpretation, India map and MCQs.

- 2026 Q4–Q12: All nine theory families were offered; absence-based rotation cannot rank them.

- 2027 specimen Q1–Q2; Q4–Q12: Topo interpretation and India-map marking/identification; the same nine theory units, with data, pictures and reasons.

## Practice content

178 original questions/variants across 12 named families. Twenty fixed papers. These are practice selections, not calibrated forecasts. Numerical variants are not copied from Selina, Goyal or another textbook.

The practice folder has question-only HTML files and separate worked-solution/model-answer HTML files. Images are embedded so a downloaded HTML file remains usable away from the site. Both versions print to PDF. On the website answers unlock only after marking the paper complete.

Geography uses original Survey of India sheets and an India outline derived from its authoritative vector. Location overlays are schematic study guides. Physics includes labelled circuit, lever and ray-construction diagrams. Full writing models are supplied; they illustrate one valid response rather than compulsory wording.
