(1) [5]
A potato strip initially has mass 7.1 g. After immersion in distilled water it has mass 7.5 g. Calculate percentage mass change and explain the direction of water movement through the cell membrane. Assume no tissue loss.
Worked solution / model answer
Mass change=0.4 g; percentage=5.63%. Water moves into cells by endosmosis from the more dilute external solution, increasing turgidity.
(2) [5]
For an X-linked recessive model of haemophilia, a mother XᴺXᴺ and father XᴺY have children. Show the cross and state risks separately for sons and daughters. If 24 births followed the expected equal sex ratio, how many affected children are expected?
Worked solution / model answer

Write the parental genotypes, list gametes and combine one allele from each parent. Count genotype and phenotype classes separately.

Mother gametes=Xᴺ; father gametes Xᴺ, Y. no affected children; no carrier daughters. Overall affected fraction=0; expected affected=0. The probability is per birth, not a fixed family outcome.
(3) [5]
In a potometer, an air bubble moves from 11 mm to 75 mm in 5 minutes. Calculate uptake distance per minute. Under more humid air it moves only 21.333 mm in the same time. Explain the difference and why a potometer measures uptake, not transpiration exactly.
Worked solution / model answer
First uptake rate=64/5=12.80 mm/min; humid-air rate=4.27 mm/min. Humidity reduces the water-vapour diffusion gradient. Some absorbed water is retained or used, so uptake is an estimate of transpiration, not an identical quantity.
(4) [5]
A school case study follows 2600 people; 20% have thyroid enlargement in an iodine-deficient region. Calculate the affected number and explain the link between iodine, thyroxine and thyroid enlargement. Why is every goitre not simply a label for excessive thyroxine?
Worked solution / model answer
Affected=520. Iodine is needed for thyroxine synthesis; deficiency may reduce hormone production and cause compensatory gland enlargement. Enlargement describes structure, not a unique hormone level; the underlying condition matters.
(5) [5]
A water plant releases 54 bubbles in 7 minutes under one light condition and 72 bubbles in the same time under brighter light. Calculate both rates, infer the effect of light and give one reason bubble count is only an approximate measure of photosynthesis.
Worked solution / model answer
Rates=7.71 and 10.29 bubbles/min. The higher rate is consistent with increased photosynthesis when light was limiting. Bubble sizes vary and some oxygen dissolves; keep plant size, temperature and CO₂ supply controlled.

3 [10]
Fertiliser runoff enters a lake. An algal bloom is followed by falling dissolved oxygen. A nearby school generates peels, paper and used batteries.
(1) [1]
Name excessive nutrient enrichment of a water body.
Worked solution / model answer
Eutrophication.
(2) [2]
Explain why oxygen falls after the bloom.
Worked solution / model answer
Dead organic material is decomposed by microorganisms; their aerobic respiration consumes dissolved oxygen.
(3) [2]
Distinguish biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste with examples.
Worked solution / model answer
Peels are broken down biologically; plastic wrappers persist under ordinary conditions and are non-biodegradable.
(4) [2]
Suggest two measures to reduce fertiliser runoff.
Worked solution / model answer
Controlled quantity/timing of fertiliser; vegetated buffers or runoff interception before it enters the lake.
(5) [3]
Give separate management routes for peels, paper and batteries.
Worked solution / model answer
Peels: compost/appropriate biogas process. Clean paper: reuse/recycle. Batteries: authorised separate collection/recovery. Segregation avoids contamination and improves recovery.
4 [10]
Tallness T is dominant over dwarfness t in a pea-plant model. Two heterozygotes are crossed. Use Mendelian segregation and a large population.
(1) [1]
Name the alternative form of a gene.
Worked solution / model answer
Allele.
(2) [2]
Write parental genotypes and their gametes.
Worked solution / model answer
Tt×Tt; each parent produces T and t.
(3) [2]
Give genotypic and phenotypic ratios.
Worked solution / model answer
TT:Tt:tt=1:2:1; tall:dwarf=3:1.
(4) [2]
Calculate the expected dwarf number among 72 offspring. Explain whether this number is guaranteed.
Worked solution / model answer
72/4=18. It is an expectation; random segregation can give a different actual count.
(5) [3]
Explain a test cross distinguishing TT and Tt, including what an all-tall small sample can establish.
Worked solution / model answer
Cross with tt. TT gives all Tt; Tt gives expected 1:1 tall:dwarf. A dwarf demonstrates a t allele in the tall parent; an all-tall small sample does not conclusively establish TT.
5 [10]
A model heart beats 75 times/min with stroke volume 73 mL.
(1) [1]
Name the natural pacemaker.
Worked solution / model answer
Sinoatrial node.
(2) [2]
Distinguish pulmonary artery and pulmonary veins.
Worked solution / model answer
Artery: deoxygenated blood from right ventricle to lungs. Veins: oxygenated blood from lungs to left atrium.
(3) [2]
Why is the left ventricle more muscular than the right?
Worked solution / model answer
It generates greater pressure for the systemic circuit; the right pumps through the shorter lower-pressure pulmonary circuit.
(4) [2]
Calculate cardiac output in L/min.
Worked solution / model answer
75×73=5475 mL/min=5.475 L/min.
(5) [3]
Trace blood from the venae cavae to the aorta, naming the chambers in order.
Worked solution / model answer
Venae cavae → right atrium → tricuspid valve → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary veins → left atrium → bicuspid valve → left ventricle → aorta. Credit chamber order, lung circuit and systemic exit.
6 [10]
One diploid cell with 24 chromosomes completes 4 successive rounds of mitosis. Every cell divides and none dies.
(1) [1]
Name the stage with chromosomes at the equator.
Worked solution / model answer
Metaphase.
(2) [2]
Calculate cell number and chromosome number in each daughter cell.
Worked solution / model answer
2^4=16 cells; each retains 24 chromosomes.
(3) [2]
Distinguish chromosome and chromatid at metaphase.
Worked solution / model answer
One replicated chromosome has two sister chromatids joined at a centromere; while joined they count as one chromosome.
(4) [2]
Give two functions of mitosis in a multicellular organism.
Worked solution / model answer
Growth by increasing cell number; repair/replacement of damaged or worn-out cells.
(5) [3]
Compare mitosis and meiosis using divisions, products and chromosome number.
Worked solution / model answer
Mitosis: one division, usually two cells, parental chromosome number maintained. Meiosis: two divisions, usually four haploid products, half the diploid chromosome number.
7 [10]
A potato strip increases from 24 g to 28 g in dilute solution. An identical strip loses mass in concentrated sugar solution. Both are blotted before weighing.
(1) [1]
Name the main process causing the changes.
Worked solution / model answer
Osmosis.
(2) [2]
Explain the first strip’s mass gain.
Worked solution / model answer
Water enters cells from the more dilute external solution across selectively permeable membranes, down the water-potential gradient.
(3) [2]
Describe water movement and the cell-state change in concentrated solution.
Worked solution / model answer
Water leaves the cells; turgor decreases. Sufficient water loss produces plasmolysis, with protoplast withdrawal from the wall.
(4) [2]
Calculate percentage mass gain and explain blotting.
Worked solution / model answer
Gain=(28−24)/24×100=16.67%. Blotting removes adhering solution that would alter measured mass.
(5) [3]
Give three controls for a fair comparison.
Worked solution / model answer
Comparable tissue and equal initial dimensions; same duration; same temperature. Accept equal solution volume and consistent blotting. Concentration is the variable changed.
8 [10]
A destarched plant has one leaf partly covered with opaque paper. After sunlight exposure, the leaf is tested for starch.
(1) [1]
Name the starch-testing reagent.
Worked solution / model answer
Iodine solution.
(2) [2]
Why must the plant be destarched first?
Worked solution / model answer
Previously stored starch must be removed so the result can be attributed to starch formed during the experiment.
(3) [2]
Why is alcohol heated in a water bath during the test?
Worked solution / model answer
Alcohol removes chlorophyll so the colour change can be seen; a water bath avoids direct flame near flammable alcohol.
(4) [2]
State the results expected in exposed and covered regions.
Worked solution / model answer
Exposed: blue-black. Covered: yellow-brown, provided destarching and light exclusion were effective.
(5) [3]
State the conclusion and explain why the experiment does not establish the need for carbon dioxide.
Worked solution / model answer
It supports a requirement for light in starch formation under these conditions. Carbon dioxide was not independently changed between the two regions; its role needs a separate controlled experiment.
