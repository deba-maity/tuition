# English Literature — evidence and study choices

Reviewed main-paper metadata: 2023–2026. The 2027 syllabus/specimen is the format reference. This is not an all-subject ten-year frequency audit.

## Study route

Give extra practice to the recent long-question gaps, while keeping concise notes for every prescribed text.

Original syllabus-scoped practice is arranged by skill and chapter. Large banks contain parameter and context variants of named question families, not thousands of distinct concepts.

## Evidence anchors

- 2023–2024 Q2–Q7: Old Merchant of Venice / Treasure Trove cohort: format evidence only.

- 2025 Q2, Q3: Julius Caesar III.2 (funeral speech); V.1 (farewell before battle).

- 2025 Q4, Q5; Q6, Q7: The Elevator; The Last Lesson; The Glove and the Lions; A Considerable Speck.

- 2026 Q2, Q3: Julius Caesar III.1 (Antony after assassination); V.3 (Cassius’s death).

- 2026 Q4, Q5; Q6, Q7: The Girl Who Can; The Pedestrian; When Great Trees Fall; The Power of Music.

- 2026 Q1: MCQ coverage includes With the Photographer and Haunted Houses.

- 2027 specimen Q2–Q7: III.2; V.1; The Elevator; The Last Lesson; The Glove and the Lions; A Considerable Speck. These also occurred as 2025 long selections, so specimen titles cannot be treated as a no-repeat shortlist.

## Practice content

104 original questions/variants across 13 named families. Twenty fixed papers. These are practice selections, not calibrated forecasts. Numerical variants are not copied from Selina, Goyal or another textbook.

The practice folder has question-only HTML files and separate worked-solution/model-answer HTML files. Images are embedded so a downloaded HTML file remains usable away from the site. Both versions print to PDF. On the website answers unlock only after marking the paper complete.

Geography uses original Survey of India sheets and an India outline derived from its authoritative vector. Location overlays are schematic study guides. Physics includes labelled circuit, lever and ray-construction diagrams. Full writing models are supplied; they illustrate one valid response rather than compulsory wording.
