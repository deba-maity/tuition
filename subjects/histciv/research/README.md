# History and Civics — evidence and study choices

Reviewed main-paper metadata: 2023–2026. The 2027 syllabus/specimen is the format reference. This is not an all-subject ten-year frequency audit.

## Study route

Use choice to reduce deep preparation: two Civics units plus a backup; three History clusters plus a backup. Keep Gandhi and the broader 1905–1947 sequence to a concise compulsory review unless your backup answers are weaker.

Recent papers offer recognisable 1857, international-conflict and UN/NAM clusters, making a selective long-answer route practical. The choice is any three of five History questions and any two of three Civics questions. No regulation guarantees the same chapter grouping every year. The 2026 paper also contained Gandhi and Independence/Partition choices; those can be left as unchosen long answers when three other answers are secure.

## Full long-answer preparation

- Civics: Parliament and Union Executive. Prepare membership/terms, procedures, powers, accountability and scenario-based questions.

- History cluster 1 — 1857: political, economic, socio-religious and military causes; immediate trigger; failure; Crown rule and subsequent changes.

- History cluster 2 — World Wars, Nazism and Fascism: causes, immediate triggers, Versailles, dictatorships, consequences and international order. This is a cluster, not a tiny single chapter.

- History cluster 3 — UN, its relevant organs/agencies, and NAM: composition/functions, limits, aims and non-alignment versus neutrality/isolation.

## Backup preparation

- Civics: Judiciary, especially Supreme Court/High Court jurisdiction, writs, independence and judicial review.

- History: Early and Assertive Nationalism. If a full backup question cannot be answered from that preparation, add one clearly defined later movement rather than assuming 1857/wars/UN must fill three slots.

## Lighter compulsory review

- Gandhian movements: brief cause–date–method–result tables for Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience and Quit India. Do not spend equal long-answer time here when the core route works.

- 1905–1947: retain partition of Bengal, Muslim League, Lucknow Pact, INA, Cabinet Mission/Mountbatten and Independence Act essentials for compulsory MCQs/shorts. This is a skim route, not a claim that the period has no exam relevance.

## Evidence anchors

- 2026 Q3–Q5: Parliament; Prime Minister/Cabinet; Supreme Court.

- 2026 Q6–Q10: 1857; Gandhian movements; Independence/Partition; WWI/dictatorships; UN/NAM.

- 2027 specimen Q3–Q5; Q6–Q10: Parliament, President and High Courts; 1857, nationalism, Gandhian movement, WWII and UN. Short items also use source extracts and statements.

## Practice content

77 original questions/variants across 13 named families. Twenty fixed papers. These are practice selections, not calibrated forecasts. Numerical variants are not copied from Selina, Goyal or another textbook.

The practice folder has question-only HTML files and separate worked-solution/model-answer HTML files. Images are embedded so a downloaded HTML file remains usable away from the site. Both versions print to PDF. On the website answers unlock only after marking the paper complete.

Geography uses original Survey of India sheets and an India outline derived from its authoritative vector. Location overlays are schematic study guides. Physics includes labelled circuit, lever and ray-construction diagrams. Full writing models are supplied; they illustrate one valid response rather than compulsory wording.
