(2) [4]
An observer’s eye is 1.6 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 50 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+50), so H=(x+50)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+50; x=25 m. H=25√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=25√3+1.6=44.90 m.
(3) [4]
The values 20, 40, 55 have frequencies 6, k, 10, respectively. Their mean is 455/11. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(670+40k)/(16+k).
2. Put this equal to 455/11 and cross-multiply: 455/11(16+k)=670+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (455/11−40)k=670−455/11×16.
4. k=6; total frequency=6+6+10=22.
5. Substitution gives (670+40×6)/(16+6)=455/11, confirming the stated mean.
