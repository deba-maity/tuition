The values 25, 35, 75 have frequencies 2, k, 10, respectively. Their mean is 1115/21. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(800+35k)/(12+k).
2. Put this equal to 1115/21 and cross-multiply: 1115/21(12+k)=800+35k.
3. Collect k terms: (1115/21−35)k=800−1115/21×12.
4. k=9; total frequency=2+9+10=21.
5. Substitution gives (800+35×9)/(12+9)=1115/21, confirming the stated mean.

Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[2,1]=-3×(8)+(-4)×(0)=-24.

The length of a rectangle exceeds its breadth by 11 cm and its area is 312 cm². Form and solve a quadratic equation. Hence find its perimeter.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let breadth=x cm, so length=(x+11) cm.
2. x(x+11)=312, giving x²+11x−312=0.
3. Factorise: (x−13)(x+24)=0.
4. x=13 or x=−24. A length is positive, so breadth=13 cm and length=24 cm.
