(2) [4]
For A=[[1,2],[6,8]] and B=[[6,0],[-3,2]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=1×(6)+(2)×(-3)=0.
AB[1,2]=1×(0)+(2)×(2)=4.
AB[2,1]=6×(6)+(8)×(-3)=12.
AB[2,2]=6×(0)+(8)×(2)=16.
AB=[[0, 4], [12, 16]].
BA[1,1]=6×(1)+(0)×(6)=6.
BA[1,2]=6×(2)+(0)×(8)=12.
BA[2,1]=-3×(1)+(2)×(6)=9.
BA[2,2]=-3×(2)+(2)×(8)=10.
BA=[[6, 12], [9, 10]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
