Worked solution / model answer

An observer’s eye is 1.5 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 84 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+84), so H=(x+84)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+84; x=42 m. H=42√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=42√3+1.5=74.25 m.
