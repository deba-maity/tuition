(3) [4]
For A=[[7,5],[-2,-3]] and B=[[6,8],[-3,5]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=7×(6)+(5)×(-3)=27.
AB[1,2]=7×(8)+(5)×(5)=81.
AB[2,1]=-2×(6)+(-3)×(-3)=-3.
AB[2,2]=-2×(8)+(-3)×(5)=-31.
AB=[[27, 81], [-3, -31]].
BA[1,1]=6×(7)+(8)×(-2)=26.
BA[1,2]=6×(5)+(8)×(-3)=6.
BA[2,1]=-3×(7)+(5)×(-2)=-31.
BA[2,2]=-3×(5)+(5)×(-3)=-30.
BA=[[26, 6], [-31, -30]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
