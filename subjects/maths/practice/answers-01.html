A bag contains 11 red balls, 4 blue balls and k green balls. Every ball is equally likely to be selected. On a single draw, P(red)=11/20. Find k and P(not blue).
Worked solution / model answer
1. Total balls=15+k. Thus 11/(15+k)=11/20.
2. Cross-multiply and solve: total=20; k=20−11−4=5.

4. P(not blue)=16/20=4/5.
(3) [4]
An observer’s eye is 1.5 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 54 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+54), so H=(x+54)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+54; x=27 m. H=27√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=27√3+1.5=48.27 m.

Worked solution / model answer

1. The two term equations are a+7d=38 and a+15d=70.

3. Substitute: a=38−7×(4)=10.
