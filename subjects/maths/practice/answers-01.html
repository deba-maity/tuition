(3) [4]
For A=[[-1,-3],[8,3]] and B=[[-3,8],[2,1]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=-1×(-3)+(-3)×(2)=-3.
AB[1,2]=-1×(8)+(-3)×(1)=-11.
AB[2,1]=8×(-3)+(3)×(2)=-18.
AB[2,2]=8×(8)+(3)×(1)=67.
AB=[[-3, -11], [-18, 67]].
BA[1,1]=-3×(-1)+(8)×(8)=67.
BA[1,2]=-3×(-3)+(8)×(3)=33.
BA[2,1]=2×(-1)+(1)×(8)=6.
BA[2,2]=2×(-3)+(1)×(3)=-3.
BA=[[67, 33], [6, -3]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
