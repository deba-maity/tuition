(2) [4]
The values 10, 40, 60 have frequencies 5, k, 14, respectively. Their mean is 1050/23. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(890+40k)/(19+k).
2. Put this equal to 1050/23 and cross-multiply: 1050/23(19+k)=890+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (1050/23−40)k=890−1050/23×19.
4. k=4; total frequency=5+4+14=23.
5. Substitution gives (890+40×4)/(19+4)=1050/23, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.

(3) [4]
The length of a rectangle exceeds its breadth by 5 cm and its area is 546 cm². Form and solve a quadratic equation. Hence find its perimeter.
Worked solution / model answer

2. x(x+5)=546, giving x²+5x−546=0.

(2) [4]
For A=[[8,0],[-3,-4]] and B=[[-2,4],[2,4]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=8×(-2)+(0)×(2)=-16.
AB[1,2]=8×(4)+(0)×(4)=32.
AB[2,1]=-3×(-2)+(-4)×(2)=-2.
AB[2,2]=-3×(4)+(-4)×(4)=-28.
AB=[[-16, 32], [-2, -28]].
BA[1,1]=-2×(8)+(4)×(-3)=-28.
BA[1,2]=-2×(0)+(4)×(-4)=-16.
BA[2,1]=2×(8)+(4)×(-3)=4.
BA[2,2]=2×(0)+(4)×(-4)=-16.
BA=[[-28, -16], [4, -16]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
