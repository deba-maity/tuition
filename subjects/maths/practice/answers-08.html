The values 30, 45, 70 have frequencies 4, k, 10, respectively. Their mean is 1135/21. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(820+45k)/(14+k).
2. Put this equal to 1135/21 and cross-multiply: 1135/21(14+k)=820+45k.
3. Collect k terms: (1135/21−45)k=820−1135/21×14.
4. k=7; total frequency=4+7+10=21.
5. Substitution gives (820+45×7)/(14+7)=1135/21, confirming the stated mean.

From external point P, tangent PA to a circle is 24 cm. A secant meets the circle first at B and then C, with PB=8 cm. Calculate PC and BC.
Worked solution / model answer

(3) [5]
For A=[[0,7],[-3,8]] and B=[[8,-3],[-3,-2]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=0×(8)+(7)×(-3)=-21.
AB[1,2]=0×(-3)+(7)×(-2)=-14.
AB[2,1]=-3×(8)+(8)×(-3)=-48.
AB[2,2]=-3×(-3)+(8)×(-2)=-7.
AB=[[-21, -14], [-48, -7]].
BA[1,1]=8×(0)+(-3)×(-3)=9.
BA[1,2]=8×(7)+(-3)×(8)=32.
BA[2,1]=-3×(0)+(-2)×(-3)=6.
BA[2,2]=-3×(7)+(-2)×(8)=-37.
BA=[[9, 32], [6, -37]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
