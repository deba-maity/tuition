(1) [4]
A solid cone of radius 4 cm and height 36 cm is melted into spheres of radius 2 cm. Find the maximum number of complete spheres and the volume of metal remaining, in terms of π.
Worked solution / model answer

Write the appropriate volume or exposed-area formula first. For recasting, equate original and final volumes; for surface area exclude joined faces.

1. Cone volume=(1/3)π×4²×36=192π cm³.
2. One sphere volume=(4/3)π×2³=32/3π cm³.
3. Volume ratio=18, so the maximum complete spheres=18.
4. Remaining volume=original volume−18×sphere volume=0π cm³.

Check your work
Volume uses cubic units, area uses square units. A radius is half a diameter.

(2) [4]
A=[[2,-5],[3,8]], B=[[7,-5],[2,5]]. Calculate AB, showing the row-by-column calculation for its top-right element.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=2×(7)+(-5)×(2)=4.
AB[1,2]=2×(-5)+(-5)×(5)=-35.
AB[2,1]=3×(7)+(8)×(2)=37.
AB[2,2]=3×(-5)+(8)×(5)=25.
AB=[[4, -35], [37, 25]].

Check your work

(3) [5]
The values 20, 45, 70 have frequencies 2, k, 12, respectively. Their mean is 1285/23. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(880+45k)/(14+k).
2. Put this equal to 1285/23 and cross-multiply: 1285/23(14+k)=880+45k.
3. Collect k terms: (1285/23−45)k=880−1285/23×14.
4. k=9; total frequency=2+9+12=23.
5. Substitution gives (880+45×9)/(14+9)=1285/23, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.
