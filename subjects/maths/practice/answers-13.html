For A=[[4,1],[0,4]] and B=[[-4,-3],[7,-1]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=4×(-4)+(1)×(7)=-9.
AB[1,2]=4×(-3)+(1)×(-1)=-13.
AB[2,1]=0×(-4)+(4)×(7)=28.
AB[2,2]=0×(-3)+(4)×(-1)=-4.
AB=[[-9, -13], [28, -4]].
BA[1,1]=-4×(4)+(-3)×(0)=-16.
BA[1,2]=-4×(1)+(-3)×(4)=-16.
BA[2,1]=7×(4)+(-1)×(0)=28.
BA[2,2]=7×(1)+(-1)×(4)=3.
BA=[[-16, -16], [28, 3]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

The values 20, 40, 70 have frequencies 3, k, 10, respectively. Their mean is 920/17. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(760+40k)/(13+k).
2. Put this equal to 920/17 and cross-multiply: 920/17(13+k)=760+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (920/17−40)k=760−920/17×13.
