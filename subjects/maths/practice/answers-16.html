Worked solution / model answer

(1) [4]
The values 30, 40, 60 have frequencies 3, k, 14, respectively. Their mean is 1410/29. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(930+40k)/(17+k).
2. Put this equal to 1410/29 and cross-multiply: 1410/29(17+k)=930+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (1410/29−40)k=930−1410/29×17.
4. k=12; total frequency=3+12+14=29.
5. Substitution gives (930+40×12)/(17+12)=1410/29, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.

(2) [4]
P divides A(-1,-1) and B(5,3) internally in the ratio AP:PB=3:5. Find P and the equation of the line through P perpendicular to AB.
Worked solution / model answer

2. xP=(5×-1+3×5)/8=5/4; yP=(5×-1+3×3)/8=1/2.

(3) [5]
An observer’s eye is 1.6 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 62 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+62), so H=(x+62)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+62; x=31 m. H=31√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=31√3+1.6=55.29 m.

(3) [4]
For A=[[-4,2],[7,3]] and B=[[-2,2],[5,2]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=-4×(-2)+(2)×(5)=18.
AB[1,2]=-4×(2)+(2)×(2)=-4.
AB[2,1]=7×(-2)+(3)×(5)=1.
AB[2,2]=7×(2)+(3)×(2)=20.
AB=[[18, -4], [1, 20]].
BA[1,1]=-2×(-4)+(2)×(7)=22.
BA[1,2]=-2×(2)+(2)×(3)=2.
BA[2,1]=5×(-4)+(2)×(7)=-6.
BA[2,2]=5×(2)+(2)×(3)=16.
BA=[[22, 2], [-6, 16]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
