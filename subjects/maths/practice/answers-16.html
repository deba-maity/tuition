Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(1245+45k)/(21+k).
2. Put this equal to 435/8 and cross-multiply: 435/8(21+k)=1245+45k.

(3) [5]
A solid hemisphere of radius 12 cm is melted and recast into identical solid cones of radius 4 cm and height 12 cm, with no metal loss. Find the number of cones and the curved surface area of one cone. Leave the area in terms of π.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Hemisphere volume=(2/3)πR³=(2/3)π×12³.
2. One cone volume=(1/3)πr²h=(1/3)π×4²×12.
3. Number=hemisphere volume/cone volume=2×12³/(4²×12)=18.

(3) [4]
For A=[[-2,7],[0,-4]] and B=[[8,5],[0,-4]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=-2×(8)+(7)×(0)=-16.
AB[1,2]=-2×(5)+(7)×(-4)=-38.
AB[2,1]=0×(8)+(-4)×(0)=0.
AB[2,2]=0×(5)+(-4)×(-4)=16.
AB=[[-16, -38], [0, 16]].
BA[1,1]=8×(-2)+(5)×(0)=-16.
BA[1,2]=8×(7)+(5)×(-4)=36.
BA[2,1]=0×(-2)+(-4)×(0)=0.
BA[2,2]=0×(7)+(-4)×(-4)=16.
BA=[[-16, 36], [0, 16]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
