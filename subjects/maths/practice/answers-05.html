(3) [5]
An observer’s eye is 1.6 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 50 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+50), so H=(x+50)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+50; x=25 m. H=25√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=25√3+1.6=44.90 m.
