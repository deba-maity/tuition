(1) [4]
For A=[[0,3],[3,-4]] and B=[[-2,6],[1,-5]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=0×(-2)+(3)×(1)=3.
AB[1,2]=0×(6)+(3)×(-5)=-15.
AB[2,1]=3×(-2)+(-4)×(1)=-10.
AB[2,2]=3×(6)+(-4)×(-5)=38.
AB=[[3, -15], [-10, 38]].
BA[1,1]=-2×(0)+(6)×(3)=18.
BA[1,2]=-2×(3)+(6)×(-4)=-30.
BA[2,1]=1×(0)+(-5)×(3)=-15.
BA[2,2]=1×(3)+(-5)×(-4)=23.
BA=[[18, -30], [-15, 23]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
