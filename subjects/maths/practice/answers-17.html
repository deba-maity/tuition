A solid cone of radius 7 cm and height 15 cm is melted into spheres of radius 1 cm. Find the maximum number of complete spheres and the volume of metal remaining, in terms of π.
Worked solution / model answer

Write the appropriate volume or exposed-area formula first. For recasting, equate original and final volumes; for surface area exclude joined faces.

1. Cone volume=(1/3)π×7²×15=245π cm³.
2. One sphere volume=(4/3)π×1³=4/3π cm³.
3. Volume ratio=735/4, so the maximum complete spheres=183.
4. Remaining volume=original volume−183×sphere volume=1π cm³.

Check your work
Volume uses cubic units, area uses square units. A radius is half a diameter.

(2) [4]
The values 25, 40, 60 have frequencies 6, k, 14, respectively. Their mean is 1110/23. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(990+40k)/(20+k).
2. Put this equal to 1110/23 and cross-multiply: 1110/23(20+k)=990+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (1110/23−40)k=990−1110/23×20.
4. k=3; total frequency=6+3+14=23.
5. Substitution gives (990+40×3)/(20+3)=1110/23, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.

A=[[1,4],[1,5]], B=[[-1,2],[0,4]]. Calculate AB, showing the row-by-column calculation for its top-right element.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=1×(-1)+(4)×(0)=-1.
AB[1,2]=1×(2)+(4)×(4)=18.
AB[2,1]=1×(-1)+(5)×(0)=-1.

Check your work
