(1) [4]
The values 20, 45, 75 have frequencies 5, k, 10, respectively. Their mean is 985/18. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(850+45k)/(15+k).
2. Put this equal to 985/18 and cross-multiply: 985/18(15+k)=850+45k.
3. Collect k terms: (985/18−45)k=850−985/18×15.
4. k=3; total frequency=5+3+10=18.
5. Substitution gives (850+45×3)/(15+3)=985/18, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.

(3) [4]
For A=[[6,-5],[-5,5]] and B=[[-4,0],[4,-1]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=6×(-4)+(-5)×(4)=-44.
AB[1,2]=6×(0)+(-5)×(-1)=5.
AB[2,1]=-5×(-4)+(5)×(4)=40.
AB[2,2]=-5×(0)+(5)×(-1)=-5.
AB=[[-44, 5], [40, -5]].
BA[1,1]=-4×(6)+(0)×(-5)=-24.
BA[1,2]=-4×(-5)+(0)×(5)=20.
BA[2,1]=4×(6)+(-1)×(-5)=29.
BA[2,2]=4×(-5)+(-1)×(5)=-25.
