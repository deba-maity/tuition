3. Slope AB=(7−(4))/(-3−(-7))=3/4.

(3) [4]
For A=[[0,8],[6,2]] and B=[[4,-5],[-3,8]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=0×(4)+(8)×(-3)=-24.
AB[1,2]=0×(-5)+(8)×(8)=64.
AB[2,1]=6×(4)+(2)×(-3)=18.
AB[2,2]=6×(-5)+(2)×(8)=-14.
AB=[[-24, 64], [18, -14]].
BA[1,1]=4×(0)+(-5)×(6)=-30.
BA[1,2]=4×(8)+(-5)×(2)=22.
BA[2,1]=-3×(0)+(8)×(6)=48.
BA[2,2]=-3×(8)+(8)×(2)=-8.
BA=[[-30, 22], [48, -8]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
