(2) [4]
For A=[[1,2],[0,-4]] and B=[[4,8],[4,6]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=1×(4)+(2)×(4)=12.
AB[1,2]=1×(8)+(2)×(6)=20.
AB[2,1]=0×(4)+(-4)×(4)=-16.
AB[2,2]=0×(8)+(-4)×(6)=-24.
AB=[[12, 20], [-16, -24]].
BA[1,1]=4×(1)+(8)×(0)=4.
BA[1,2]=4×(2)+(8)×(-4)=-24.
BA[2,1]=4×(1)+(6)×(0)=4.
BA[2,2]=4×(2)+(6)×(-4)=-16.
BA=[[4, -24], [4, -16]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
(3) [4]
The values 20, 35, 70 have frequencies 2, k, 11, respectively. Their mean is 246/5. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(810+35k)/(13+k).
2. Put this equal to 246/5 and cross-multiply: 246/5(13+k)=810+35k.
3. Collect k terms: (246/5−35)k=810−246/5×13.
4. k=12; total frequency=2+12+11=25.
5. Substitution gives (810+35×12)/(13+12)=246/5, confirming the stated mean.

An observer’s eye is 1.5 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 24 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+24), so H=(x+24)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+24; x=12 m. H=12√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=12√3+1.5=22.28 m.
