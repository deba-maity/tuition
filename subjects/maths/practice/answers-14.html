(2) [4]
A=[[-2,0],[-1,5]], B=[[1,-3],[-1,0]]. Calculate AB, showing the row-by-column calculation for its top-right element.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=-2×(1)+(0)×(-1)=-2.
AB[1,2]=-2×(-3)+(0)×(0)=6.
AB[2,1]=-1×(1)+(5)×(-1)=-6.
AB[2,2]=-1×(-3)+(5)×(0)=3.
AB=[[-2, 6], [-6, 3]].

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.

(3) [4]
The values 30, 40, 60 have frequencies 3, k, 12, respectively. Their mean is 155/3. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(810+40k)/(15+k).
2. Put this equal to 155/3 and cross-multiply: 155/3(15+k)=810+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (155/3−40)k=810−155/3×15.
4. k=3; total frequency=3+3+12=18.
5. Substitution gives (810+40×3)/(15+3)=155/3, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.
