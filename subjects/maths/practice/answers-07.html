(3) [4]
For A=[[7,-4],[1,6]] and B=[[3,-4],[7,5]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=7×(3)+(-4)×(7)=-7.
AB[1,2]=7×(-4)+(-4)×(5)=-48.
AB[2,1]=1×(3)+(6)×(7)=45.
AB[2,2]=1×(-4)+(6)×(5)=26.
AB=[[-7, -48], [45, 26]].
BA[1,1]=3×(7)+(-4)×(1)=17.
BA[1,2]=3×(-4)+(-4)×(6)=-36.
BA[2,1]=7×(7)+(5)×(1)=54.
BA[2,2]=7×(-4)+(5)×(6)=2.
BA=[[17, -36], [54, 2]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.

(3) [5]
An observer’s eye is 1.5 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 96 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+96), so H=(x+96)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+96; x=48 m. H=48√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=48√3+1.5=84.64 m.

(3) [4]
The values 25, 40, 55 have frequencies 4, k, 9, respectively. Their mean is 715/16. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(595+40k)/(13+k).
2. Put this equal to 715/16 and cross-multiply: 715/16(13+k)=595+40k.
3. Collect k terms: (715/16−40)k=595−715/16×13.
4. k=3; total frequency=4+3+9=16.
5. Substitution gives (595+40×3)/(13+3)=715/16, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.
