(3) [4]
The values 25, 50, 75 have frequencies 6, k, 8, respectively. Their mean is 625/12. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer

For a frequency distribution calculate Σf and Σfx separately, then mean=Σfx/Σf. For grouped data use class marks or the stated interpolation rule.

1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(750+50k)/(14+k).
2. Put this equal to 625/12 and cross-multiply: 625/12(14+k)=750+50k.
3. Collect k terms: (625/12−50)k=750−625/12×14.
4. k=10; total frequency=6+10+8=24.
5. Substitution gives (750+50×10)/(14+10)=625/12, confirming the stated mean.

Check your work
Do not take an unweighted average of values with unequal frequencies.

(3) [4]
For A=[[1,0],[-5,1]] and B=[[8,-5],[-5,4]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=1×(8)+(0)×(-5)=8.
AB[1,2]=1×(-5)+(0)×(4)=-5.
AB[2,1]=-5×(8)+(1)×(-5)=-45.
AB[2,2]=-5×(-5)+(1)×(4)=29.
AB=[[8, -5], [-45, 29]].
BA[1,1]=8×(1)+(-5)×(-5)=33.
BA[1,2]=8×(0)+(-5)×(1)=-5.
BA[2,1]=-5×(1)+(4)×(-5)=-25.
BA[2,2]=-5×(0)+(4)×(1)=4.
BA=[[33, -5], [-25, 4]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
