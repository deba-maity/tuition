(3) [4]
For A=[[-2,7],[0,-4]] and B=[[8,5],[0,-4]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=-2×(8)+(7)×(0)=-16.
AB[1,2]=-2×(5)+(7)×(-4)=-38.
AB[2,1]=0×(8)+(-4)×(0)=0.
AB[2,2]=0×(5)+(-4)×(-4)=16.
AB=[[-16, -38], [0, 16]].
BA[1,1]=8×(-2)+(5)×(0)=-16.
BA[1,2]=8×(7)+(5)×(-4)=36.
BA[2,1]=0×(-2)+(-4)×(0)=0.
BA[2,2]=0×(7)+(-4)×(-4)=16.
BA=[[-16, 36], [0, 16]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
