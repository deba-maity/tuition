Write the appropriate volume or exposed-area formula first. For recasting, equate original and final volumes; for surface area exclude joined faces.

(2) [4]
An observer’s eye is 1.8 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 28 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+28), so H=(x+28)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+28; x=14 m. H=14√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=14√3+1.8=26.05 m.
(3) [4]
A=[[-5,7],[7,8]], B=[[1,2],[1,6]]. Calculate AB, showing the row-by-column calculation for its top-right element.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=-5×(1)+(7)×(1)=2.
AB[1,2]=-5×(2)+(7)×(6)=32.
AB[2,1]=7×(1)+(8)×(1)=15.
