(1) [4]
For A=[[2,2],[-2,1]] and B=[[-3,4],[8,-4]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=2×(-3)+(2)×(8)=10.
AB[1,2]=2×(4)+(2)×(-4)=0.
AB[2,1]=-2×(-3)+(1)×(8)=14.
AB[2,2]=-2×(4)+(1)×(-4)=-12.
AB=[[10, 0], [14, -12]].
BA[1,1]=-3×(2)+(4)×(-2)=-14.
BA[1,2]=-3×(2)+(4)×(1)=-2.
BA[2,1]=8×(2)+(-4)×(-2)=24.
BA[2,2]=8×(2)+(-4)×(1)=12.
BA=[[-14, -2], [24, 12]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.
(2) [4]
The values 20, 35, 70 have frequencies 3, k, 8, respectively. Their mean is 970/21. Find k and the total frequency.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Mean=Σfx/Σf=(620+35k)/(11+k).
2. Put this equal to 970/21 and cross-multiply: 970/21(11+k)=620+35k.
3. Collect k terms: (970/21−35)k=620−970/21×11.
4. k=10; total frequency=3+10+8=21.
5. Substitution gives (620+35×10)/(11+10)=970/21, confirming the stated mean.
