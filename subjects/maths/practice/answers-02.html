An observer’s eye is 1.5 m above level ground. The elevation of a tower top is 30°. After moving 24 m directly towards its base, the elevation is 60°. Find the tower’s height, correct to two decimal places.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let the nearer horizontal distance be x m and height above eye level be H m.
2. From the nearer position, tan60°=H/x, so H=x√3.
3. From the farther position, tan30°=H/(x+24), so H=(x+24)/√3.
4. Equate: 3x=x+24; x=12 m. H=12√3 m.
5. Tower height=H+eye height=12√3+1.5=22.28 m.

(3) [4]
For A=[[7,-4],[1,6]] and B=[[3,-4],[7,5]], calculate AB and BA. Decide whether these particular matrices commute.
Worked solution / model answer

For AB, multiply each row of A by each column of B. The entry (i,j) is the sum of the corresponding products.

AB[1,1]=7×(3)+(-4)×(7)=-7.
AB[1,2]=7×(-4)+(-4)×(5)=-48.
AB[2,1]=1×(3)+(6)×(7)=45.
AB[2,2]=1×(-4)+(6)×(5)=26.
AB=[[-7, -48], [45, 26]].
BA[1,1]=3×(7)+(-4)×(1)=17.
BA[1,2]=3×(-4)+(-4)×(6)=-36.
BA[2,1]=7×(7)+(5)×(1)=54.
BA[2,2]=7×(-4)+(5)×(6)=2.
BA=[[17, -36], [54, 2]].
They do not commute because AB≠BA.

Check your work
Order matters: AB need not equal BA. For unknown entries, equate corresponding positions.
