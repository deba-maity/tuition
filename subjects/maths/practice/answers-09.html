The length of a rectangle exceeds its breadth by 5 cm and its area is 546 cm². Form and solve a quadratic equation. Hence find its perimeter.
Worked solution / model answer
1. Let breadth=x cm, so length=(x+5) cm.
2. x(x+5)=546, giving x²+5x−546=0.
3. Factorise: (x−21)(x+26)=0.
4. x=21 or x=−26. A length is positive, so breadth=21 cm and length=26 cm.
5. Perimeter=2(21+26)=94 cm.

Worked solution / model answer

1. The two term equations are a+4d=42 and a+7d=66.
2. Subtract: 3d=24, so d=8.
3. Substitute: a=42−4×(8)=10.
4. S33=33/2[2(10)+32(8)]=4554.
