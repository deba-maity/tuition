(1) [5]
Acidulated water is electrolysed with inert electrodes. 38 cm³ hydrogen is collected at one electrode. Name both electrode products, calculate oxygen volume at the same conditions, write a cathode equation and explain why acid is added.
Worked solution / model answer

Identify electrolyte and electrodes. At the cathode reduction consumes electrons; at the anode oxidation releases electrons. Write charge-balanced half-equations.

Cathode H₂; anode O₂. H₂:O₂=2:1, so oxygen=19 cm³. Cathode: 2H⁺+2e⁻→H₂. Dilute sulphuric acid supplies ions and improves conductivity; it is not the intended decomposition product.

Check your work
Copper anodes replenish Cu²⁺ in copper refining; inert anodes do not supply replacement copper ions.

(3) [4]
Atoms X and Y are in period 2, with 1 and 7 valence electrons respectively. X has mass number 13. Compare their metallic character, atomic size and electronegativity. Justify each trend across a period.
Worked solution / model answer
X is to the left of Y. Across a period effective nuclear attraction generally rises: atomic size decreases, electronegativity increases and metallic character decreases. Thus X is generally larger and more metallic, Y more electronegative. The supplied mass number does not reverse periodic position.
