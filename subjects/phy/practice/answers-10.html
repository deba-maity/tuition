(3) [4]
A 78-turn coil gives a deflection while a bar magnet approaches it. In one trial speed rises from 2 to 4 cm/s. Predict the effect on induced emf. Explain the effect of each further change: (a) move the magnet faster; (b) keep the magnet stationary.
