(7) [3]
A 500 g sample with specific heat capacity 0.4 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹ is heated from 23°C to 61°C without a change of state. Find heat supplied and the sample’s heat capacity.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Q=mcΔT=500×0.4×38=7600 J. Heat capacity=mc=200 J/°C. Specific heat capacity is per unit mass, whereas heat capacity is for the entire sample.

(3) [4]
A convex lens has focal length 29 cm. An object of height 5 cm is placed 58 cm from the lens, at 2F₁. Complete the two principal rays, locate the image, and calculate image distance and height. State its nature.
Worked solution / model answer

Use the Cartesian sign convention: real object u<0, convex f>0 and concave f<0. Apply 1/v−1/u=1/f and magnification m=v/u.

1. Draw one ray parallel to the principal axis; after refraction it passes through F₂. Draw a second through O, undeviated. Their intersection is at 2F₂.
2. u=−58 cm, f=+29 cm. Lens formula gives 1/v=1/29−1/58=1/58; v=+58 cm.
3. Magnification=v/u=−1. Image height=−5 cm: equal magnitude and inverted.
4. The image is real, inverted and the same size as the object.
