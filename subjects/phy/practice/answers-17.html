A perpendicular force 89 N has a moment arm 30 cm. Find its moment.
  1. 30.705 N m
  2. 26.7 N m
  3. 22.695 N m
  4. 34.71 N m
Worked solution / model answer
B. 26.7 N m

(2) [3]
A 200 g sample with specific heat capacity 0.5 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹ is heated from 25°C to 46°C without a change of state. Find heat supplied and the sample’s heat capacity.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Q=mcΔT=200×0.5×21=2100 J. Heat capacity=mc=100 J/°C. Specific heat capacity is per unit mass, whereas heat capacity is for the entire sample.
