A perpendicular force 73 N has a moment arm 19 cm. Find its moment.
  1. 15.951 N m
  2. 18.031 N m
  3. 11.79 N m
  4. 13.87 N m
Worked solution / model answer
D. 13.87 N m
Perpendicular distance=0.19 m. Moment=force×distance=73×0.19=13.87 N m. At half the distance the moment is 6.935 N m. In the inverse calculation force=13.87/0.19=73 N; through the pivot the arm is zero.
