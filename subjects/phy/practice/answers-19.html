(3) [4]
150 g of water at 67°C is mixed with 100 g of water at 11°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Heat lost=heat gained: 150c(67−T)=100c(T−11). T=(150×67+100×11)/250=44.60°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.
