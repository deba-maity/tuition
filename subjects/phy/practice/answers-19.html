(2) [3]
A constant 15 N force acts while an object is displaced 34 m. The angle between force and displacement is 90°. Find the work and interpret its sign.
Worked solution / model answer

W=Fs cosθ=15×34×cos 90°=0 J. The force is perpendicular to displacement and does no work.
(3) [4]
150 g of water at 94°C is mixed with 200 g of water at 29°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Heat lost=heat gained: 150c(94−T)=200c(T−29). T=(150×94+200×29)/350=56.86°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.

A nucleus has mass number 150 and atomic number 67. It emits 1 alpha particle(s) and then 3 beta-minus particle(s). Find the final mass and atomic numbers and explain whether a following gamma emission changes either.
Worked solution / model answer

Worked solution / model answer
sin C=1/μ=1/1.5, so C=41.8°. Since i is greater than C, the ray undergoes total internal reflection. The dense-to-rare condition is satisfied.
(3) [4]
A 27-turn coil gives a deflection while a bar magnet approaches it. In one trial speed rises from 1 to 5 cm/s. Predict the effect on induced emf. Explain the effect of each further change: (a) move the magnet faster; (b) double the turns.
Worked solution / model answer
Faster approach increases the rate of flux change and induced emf. (a) A larger rate of change of magnetic flux produces a larger induced emf. (b) For the same flux change per turn, the induced emf increases.
