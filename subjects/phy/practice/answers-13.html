u=-3.5 cm, f=14 cm. 1/v−1/u=1/f gives v=-4.67 cm. m=v/u=1.33. Image is virtual, erect. A negative v places it on the object side.

(1) [3]
A 900 g sample with specific heat capacity 4.2 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹ is heated from 25°C to 77°C without a change of state. Find heat supplied and the sample’s heat capacity.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Q=mcΔT=900×4.2×52=196560 J. Heat capacity=mc=3780 J/°C. Specific heat capacity is per unit mass, whereas heat capacity is for the entire sample.
(2) [3]
A body initially has kinetic energy 96 J. Its mass is multiplied by 8 and speed by 5. Find the new kinetic energy and explain why multiplying by 40 is incorrect.
Worked solution / model answer

Use work=force×distance in the force direction, GPE=mgh, KE=½mv² and power=energy/time. Efficiency=useful output/input.

KE is proportional to mv². Multiplier=8×5²=200. New KE=19200 J. Speed enters quadratically, not linearly.
