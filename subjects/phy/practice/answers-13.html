200 g of water at 90°C is mixed with 150 g of water at 25°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Heat lost=heat gained: 200c(90−T)=150c(T−25). T=(200×90+150×25)/350=62.14°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.
