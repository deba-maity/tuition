A perpendicular force 26 N has a moment arm 45 cm. Find its moment.
  1. 9.945 N m
  2. 13.455 N m
  3. 15.21 N m
  4. 11.7 N m
Worked solution / model answer
D. 11.7 N m
Perpendicular distance=0.45 m. Moment=force×distance=26×0.45=11.7 N m. At half the distance the moment is 5.85 N m. In the inverse calculation force=11.7/0.45=26 N; through the pivot the arm is zero.

(1) [2]
Why is gamma radiation undeflected by electric and magnetic fields?
Worked solution / model answer
It is uncharged electromagnetic radiation, so it experiences no charge-based deflecting force.
(2) [2]
Can a real machine have efficiency above 100 percent? Give a reason.
Worked solution / model answer
No; useful energy output cannot exceed total energy input. Real losses reduce efficiency below unity.

State the relation between wave speed, frequency and wavelength. Give the SI unit of wavelength.
Worked solution / model answer
v=fλ; metre.
(4) [2]
Define mechanical advantage and velocity ratio.
Worked solution / model answer
MA=load/effort; VR=distance moved by effort/distance moved by load.

Define moment of a force and give its SI unit.
Worked solution / model answer
Force × perpendicular distance from the pivot; newton metre (N m).

(7) [3]
Media A and B have absolute refractive indices 1.25 and 1.8. A ray passes from A into B at incidence 57°. Calculate the relative refractive index of B with respect to A and sin r. State the bending direction.
Worked solution / model answer
μBA=μB/μA=1.440. sin r=μA sin i/μB=0.582. B is optically denser, so it bends towards the normal.
