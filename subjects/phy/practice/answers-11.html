(1) [1]
A perpendicular force 26 N has a moment arm 45 cm. Find its moment.
  1. 9.945 N m
  2. 13.455 N m
  3. 15.21 N m
  4. 11.7 N m
Worked solution / model answer
D. 11.7 N m
Perpendicular distance=0.45 m. Moment=force×distance=26×0.45=11.7 N m. At half the distance the moment is 5.85 N m. In the inverse calculation force=11.7/0.45=26 N; through the pivot the arm is zero.

(7) [3]
A 27 kg body increases its speed from 5 m/s to 16 m/s. Find the increase in kinetic energy and the net work done. State the principle connecting the two.
Worked solution / model answer

(1) [3]
A source produces sound of frequency 400 Hz in air where speed is 330 m/s. Find the wavelength. When the source enters a medium in which sound travels twice as fast, what happens to frequency and wavelength?
Worked solution / model answer

(2) [3]
A coin is 15 cm below the surface of a transparent liquid of refractive index 1.25. Viewed nearly normally from air, find its apparent depth and apparent upward shift.
Worked solution / model answer
Apparent depth=real depth/μ=15/1.25=12 cm. Shift=real−apparent=3 cm. The relation assumes near-normal viewing.
(3) [4]
Two resistors of 18 Ω and 8 Ω are connected first in series and then in parallel across the same ideal 21 V battery. Calculate equivalent resistance and total current in both arrangements. Find the current through each parallel branch.
Worked solution / model answer

Identify series and parallel parts before calculating equivalent resistance. Use V=IR, P=VI=I²R=V²/R and energy=power×time.

1. Series resistance=18+8=26 Ω.
2. Series current=V/R=21/26=0.808 A.
3. Parallel resistance=R₁R₂/(R₁+R₂)=144/26=72/13 Ω.
4. Parallel total current=21/(72/13)=3.792 A.
5. Branch currents: I₁=21/18=1.167 A and I₂=21/8=2.625 A. Their sum equals the total parallel current.
