(3) [4]
A 46-turn coil gives a deflection while a bar magnet approaches it. In one trial speed rises from 3 to 5 cm/s. Predict the effect on induced emf. Explain the effect of each further change: (a) double the turns; (b) keep the magnet stationary.
Worked solution / model answer
