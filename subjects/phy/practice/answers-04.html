Worked solution / model answer

The measured delay includes travel to the reflector and back; d=vt/2.

Distinguish kinetic and gravitational potential energy.
Worked solution / model answer
Energy due to motion, ½mv²; energy due to height in a gravitational field, mgh near Earth.

(7) [3]
A heater supplies 60 W entirely to 1000 g material of specific heat 0.9 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹. How long does a temperature rise of 31°C take, if no phase change occurs?
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Q=mcΔT=27900 J. Since P=Q/t, t=Q/P=465.00 s. Watt is joule per second.
