(2) [3]
A source produces sound of frequency 125 Hz in air where speed is 320 m/s. Find the wavelength. When the source enters a medium in which sound travels twice as fast, what happens to frequency and wavelength?
Worked solution / model answer
λ=v/f=320/125=2.560 m. Frequency is fixed by the source and remains 125 Hz. At twice the speed, wavelength doubles to 5.120 m.
(3) [4]
300 g water at 30°C is mixed with 500 g ice at 0°C in an insulated vessel. With cwater=4.2 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹ and L=336 J/g, determine whether all the ice melts, the final temperature, and the mass of ice remaining.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Water cooling to 0°C supplies 37800 J. This melts 112.50 g, less than 500 g. Ice remains, so equilibrium temperature is 0°C. Remaining ice=387.50 g. Do not use a liquid-only average-temperature formula.

(3) [4]
A light horizontal beam is supported at a pivot. A 10 N load is 10 cm to the left. What downward force at 15 cm to the right balances it? Find the upward reaction at the pivot.
Worked solution / model answer
Clockwise moment=anticlockwise moment: F×15=10×10. F=20/3 N. Vertical balance gives reaction=10+20/3=50/3 N. The beam weight is neglected. The added weight has no moment about the pivot, so the balancing force is unchanged; the upward reaction increases by 21 N.
