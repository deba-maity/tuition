(7) [3]
A coin is 39 cm below the surface of a transparent liquid of refractive index 1.6. Viewed nearly normally from air, find its apparent depth and apparent upward shift.

200 g of water at 72°C is mixed with 200 g of water at 14°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Heat lost=heat gained: 200c(72−T)=200c(T−14). T=(200×72+200×14)/400=43.00°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.
