Light must go from optically denser to rarer medium; angle of incidence must exceed the critical angle.

(7) [3]
A nucleus has mass number 100 and atomic number 35. It emits 1 alpha particle(s) and then 4 beta-minus particle(s). Find the final mass and atomic numbers and explain whether a following gamma emission changes either.

200 g of water at 92°C is mixed with 150 g of water at 16°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Heat lost=heat gained: 200c(92−T)=150c(T−16). T=(200×92+150×16)/350=59.43°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.
