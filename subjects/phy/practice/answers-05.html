(1) [3]
Two sound waves A and B have the same frequency 900 Hz and the same waveform. B has amplitude 5 times A, whose amplitude is 1 arbitrary units. Compare pitch, quality and intensity.
Worked solution / model answer
Pitch is the same because frequency is the same. Quality is the same because waveform is the same. Intensity ratio IB:IA=25:1 since intensity is proportional to amplitude squared.
(2) [3]
A straight current-carrying wire carries 19 A in a magnetic field (N to the left and S to the right). Explain the change in force direction if you (a) reverse current only, (b) reverse current and interchange poles. Name the hand rule for the motor effect.
Worked solution / model answer
(a) The force reverses. (b) Both changes reverse their factors, so the force direction remains unchanged. Use Fleming’s left-hand rule for force on a current-carrying conductor.
(3) [4]
200 g of water at 71°C is mixed with 150 g of water at 29°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Heat lost=heat gained: 200c(71−T)=150c(T−29). T=(200×71+150×29)/350=53.00°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.
