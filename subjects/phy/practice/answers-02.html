Worked solution / model answer
Steady DC produces no continuously changing magnetic flux, so no sustained induced secondary emf; switching transients are different.

(7) [3]
A source produces sound of frequency 350 Hz in air where speed is 340 m/s. Find the wavelength. When the source enters a medium in which sound travels twice as fast, what happens to frequency and wavelength?
