A lens has focal length -46 cm, irrespective of object position 51 cm. Find power to 3 decimal places.
  1. -3.174 D
  2. -0.174 D
  3. -1.174 D
  4. -2.174 D
Worked solution / model answer

A machine has velocity ratio 4 and efficiency 60%. It is operated with effort 31 N. Find mechanical advantage and the load it can raise steadily. Why can its MA be less than its VR?
Worked solution / model answer

R=V/I=60.000 Ω. R∝L/A and A∝r², so R2/R1=4/4=1; R2=60.000 Ω.

I=P/V=7.500 A. R=V²/P=32.000 Ω. At half voltage, P′=V′²/R=P/4=450.0 W. This assumes unchanged resistance.

(3) [4]
A 500 g metal block at 91°C is put in 100 g water at 16°C. The final temperature is 23°C. Ignore the vessel and heat loss. Find the metal’s specific heat capacity; cwater=4.2 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

500c(91−23)=100×4.2×(23−16). c=0.086 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹. Use the metal’s cooling interval, not its original temperature.
