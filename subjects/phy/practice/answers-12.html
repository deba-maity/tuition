(1) [1]
A perpendicular force 12 N has a moment arm 35 cm. Find its moment.
  1. 5.2 N m
  2. 3.2 N m
  3. 4.2 N m
  4. 6.2 N m
Worked solution / model answer
C. 4.2 N m
Perpendicular distance=0.35 m. Moment=force×distance=12×0.35=4.2 N m. At half the distance the moment is 2.1 N m. In the inverse calculation force=4.2/0.35=12 N; through the pivot the arm is zero.
(2) [1]
Find the work by a 10 N force over 17 m when the force and displacement enclose 90°.
  1. -1 J
  2. 1 J
  3. 2 J
  4. 0 J
Worked solution / model answer

Use work=force×distance in the force direction, GPE=mgh, KE=½mv² and power=energy/time. Efficiency=useful output/input.

D. 0 J
W=Fs cosθ=10×17×cos 90°=0 J. The force is perpendicular to displacement and does no work.

10 g ice at 0°C is completely melted and the water is then warmed to 31°C. Calculate total heat required. Use Lfusion=336 J/g and cwater=4.2 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Worked solution / model answer

(2) [3]
A meter reads 6250 kWh initially and 6347 kWh later. A single 500 W appliance is the only load and operates continuously between readings. Find the operating time. Explain why dividing the readings themselves would be meaningless.
Worked solution / model answer

Identify series and parallel parts before calculating equivalent resistance. Use V=IR, P=VI=I²R=V²/R and energy=power×time.

Energy change=97 kWh; power=0.5 kW. Time=E/P=97/0.5=194 h. Readings are cumulative; use their difference, not their ratio.
(3) [4]
An object is 3.2 cm to the left of a convex lens of focal length magnitude 8 cm. Light travels left to right. Use Cartesian signs to find image distance, magnification and image nature.
Worked solution / model answer

Use the Cartesian sign convention: real object u<0, convex f>0 and concave f<0. Apply 1/v−1/u=1/f and magnification m=v/u.

u=-3.2 cm, f=8 cm. 1/v−1/u=1/f gives v=-5.33 cm. m=v/u=1.67. Image is virtual, erect. A negative v places it on the object side.

(2) [3]
A straight current-carrying wire carries 5 A in a magnetic field (N above and S below). Explain the change in force direction if you (a) reverse current and interchange poles, (b) reverse current only. Name the hand rule for the motor effect.
Worked solution / model answer

(3) [4]
250 g of water at 79°C is mixed with 150 g of water at 16°C in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the final temperature. State the heat-balance equation.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Heat lost=heat gained: 250c(79−T)=150c(T−16). T=(250×79+150×16)/400=55.38°C. The result lies between the starting temperatures.

A nucleus has mass number 168 and atomic number 75. It emits 2 alpha particle(s) and then 1 beta-minus particle(s). Find the final mass and atomic numbers and explain whether a following gamma emission changes either.
