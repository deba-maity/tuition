Distinguish kinetic and gravitational potential energy.
Worked solution / model answer

How does increasing frequency affect sound pitch? How does increasing amplitude affect loudness?

Can a real machine have efficiency above 100 percent? Give a reason.

No; useful energy output cannot exceed total energy input. Real losses reduce efficiency below unity.

Worked solution / model answer

(2) [3]
A 550 g sample with specific heat capacity 0.5 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹ is heated from 35°C to 64°C without a change of state. Find heat supplied and the sample’s heat capacity.
Worked solution / model answer

Write heat lost=heat gained. Use Q=mcΔT for a temperature change and Q=mL for a change of state. Check whether all ice melts before solving for a final temperature above 0°C.

Q=mcΔT=550×0.5×29=7975 J. Heat capacity=mc=275 J/°C. Specific heat capacity is per unit mass, whereas heat capacity is for the entire sample.

A nucleus has mass number 102 and atomic number 30. It emits 2 alpha particle(s) and then 2 beta-minus particle(s). Find the final mass and atomic numbers and explain whether a following gamma emission changes either.
