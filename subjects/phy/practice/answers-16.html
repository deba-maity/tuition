(1) [1]
A perpendicular force 58 N has a moment arm 33 cm. Find its moment.
  1. 16.269 N m
  2. 19.14 N m
  3. 22.011 N m
  4. 24.882 N m
Worked solution / model answer
B. 19.14 N m
Perpendicular distance=0.33 m. Moment=force×distance=58×0.33=19.14 N m. At half the distance the moment is 9.57 N m. In the inverse calculation force=19.14/0.33=58 N; through the pivot the arm is zero.

(7) [3]
