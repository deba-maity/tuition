(1) [3]
A ray travels from air into a medium of refractive index 1.6. If sin i=0.37, find sin r and the speed of light in the medium. Use c=3×10⁸ m/s.
Worked solution / model answer
Snell’s law gives sin r=sin i/μ=0.37/1.6=0.231. v=c/μ=1.875e+08 m/s. The ray bends towards the normal.
(2) [3]
A straight current-carrying wire carries 1 A in a magnetic field (N to the left and S to the right). Explain the change in force direction if you (a) reverse current and interchange poles, (b) interchange the magnetic poles only. Name the hand rule for the motor effect.
Worked solution / model answer
(a) Both changes reverse their factors, so the force direction remains unchanged. (b) The force reverses. Use Fleming’s left-hand rule for force on a current-carrying conductor.
(3) [4]
A nucleus has mass number 100 and atomic number 25. It emits 3 alpha particle(s) and then 2 beta-minus particle(s). Find the final mass and atomic numbers and explain whether a following gamma emission changes either.
Worked solution / model answer
Each α reduces A by 4 and Z by 2; each β⁻ leaves A unchanged and increases Z by 1. Final A=88; Z=21. Gamma emission changes neither A nor Z. Element identity is set by atomic number, which changes during beta-minus emission.
