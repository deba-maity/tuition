# Physics — evidence and study choices

Reviewed main-paper metadata: 2023–2026. The 2027 syllabus/specimen is the format reference. This is not an all-subject ten-year frequency audit.

## Study route

Alternate conceptual reasons with calculations so formula recall is not mistaken for understanding.

Original syllabus-scoped practice is arranged by skill and chapter. Large banks contain parameter and context variants of named question families, not thousands of distinct concepts.

## Evidence anchors

- 2026 Q4–Q5: Optics/spectrum; lens and calorimetry/cooling curve.

- 2026 Q6–Q7: Moments/energy; machines/sound.

- 2026 Q8–Q9: Current/household electricity; electrical energy/electromagnetic induction/nuclear changes.

- 2027 specimen Q3(i); Q4–Q9: A lens made from two materials; refraction, thermal graphs, moments/machines, circuits and nuclear/electromagnetic tasks. Practise interpreting unfamiliar arrangements.

## Practice content

2100 original questions/variants across 52 named families. Twenty fixed papers. These are practice selections, not calibrated forecasts. Numerical variants are not copied from Selina, Goyal or another textbook.

The practice folder has question-only HTML files and separate worked-solution/model-answer HTML files. Images are embedded so a downloaded HTML file remains usable away from the site. Both versions print to PDF. On the website answers unlock only after marking the paper complete.

Geography uses original Survey of India sheets and an India outline derived from its authoritative vector. Location overlays are schematic study guides. Physics includes labelled circuit, lever and ray-construction diagrams. Full writing models are supplied; they illustrate one valid response rather than compulsory wording.
