INFORMAL LETTER- Sample



 32 Hadapsar Road,

 Hadapsar,

 Pune - 411028.



 14th February 2026.



 Dear Grandfather,

 I hope you are doing well and in good health. I am excited to share with you a

 wonderful day I recently spent playing outdoor games with my friends. I

 especially wanted to thank you and tell you about it because you have always

 encouraged me to go outside, be active, and enjoy sports instead of staying

 indoors all the time.

 Last Sunday, my friends and I decided to spend the entire day at the

 playground in our neighbourhood. We started the morning with a game of

 cricket. I remembered how you once told me that teamwork and patience are

 very important in games. While playing, I tried to focus on coordinating with

 my teammates and encouraging them, even when we lost a wicket. After

 cricket, we ended the day with a few rounds of badminton. Running around in

 the fresh air, feeling the sunshine, and hearing everyone laugh made the day

 truly special.

 At first, we were all very competitive, and there were small disagreements

 about rules and scores. But we soon realised that enjoying the game mattered

 more than winning. By the end of the day, we were cheering for each other

 and celebrating every small success together. I felt energetic, happy, and proud

 of myself for staying active the whole day.

 This experience taught me many valuable lessons. I learned the importance of
